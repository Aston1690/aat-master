# Static post review

Read before work. Review the set together, then every exported post at full and feed/phone size. Record pass, fail, not tested or not applicable with filenames and evidence in project notes.

- Every static post contains a prominent relevant photograph or substantive illustration; no text-only post or token icon/logo/shape stands in for imagery.
- Image sources or generation prompts and saved assets are recorded. Generated scenes are not passed off as documentary evidence of real client people, places or events.
- Review scope matches the request. Screenshots, metadata and exports contain no accidental credentials or private information. Approval and factual verification are recorded separately.
- Count, canvas, actual format, extension and colour mode match the brief; each post is a separate final image.
- Standalone posts have no unwanted carousel numbers or swipe labels. On-image copy is distinguished from the accompanying caption.
- Exact approved wording, punctuation, URL, CTA and legal copy survive. Claims, dates, prices, quotes and proof have sources; no filler labels were invented.
- Each post answers its specific image description and has one clear visual idea. The selected medium fits the brief rather than defaulting to stock photography or UI cards.
- Brand fonts, colours, image treatment and verified logos are correct; no client-specific system has leaked from another job.
- Text groups fit measured space. Headline, supporting line, proof and attribution remain visibly connected and readable at feed size.
- Copy, logo, CTA and focal subjects have adequate contrast and clear space. No accidental overlap, clipping, weak crop or rescue patch remains.
- Images and logos retain their geometry, quality and correct background treatment. QR codes, if included, resolve to the approved destination when tested.
- Generated imagery contains no false factual detail, fake logo, malformed product or production text. Exact words and official assets were composed separately.
- Intentional negative space supports hierarchy. Decorative filler, generic dashboards, tiny labels and purposeless cards do not replace the concept.
- Image-only edits preserve the approved layout. Approved posts remain intact while another is revised; rejected work is archived.
- Full-size and feed/phone views were inspected for every final image, not merely selected edge cases. The inspected files include the latest corrections.
- Editable source, necessary assets and final exports are available. No agent posting, sending or public asset upload occurred.
- A human designer reviews each final before external handover; that review is distinct from agent inspection.

Wrong facts, altered copy, distorted logo, missing posts, unreadable content or uninspected exports prevent a pass. Correct dimensions alone are not evidence of good design.

Missing required imagery, placeholder artwork, or imagery too small or obscured to communicate at feed size prevents a pass. An earlier approval of text-only work does not waive the owner’s current imagery requirement.

## Package and handover consistency

- Every requested deliverable and variant is accounted for against the brief, with its assigned copy/source and actual export or route. Reduced scope was explicit; no universal variant count was assumed.
- Approved accompanying captions, hashtags and links are preserved and paired with the correct final files or slide set in the caption handover; absent captions are disclosed and unapproved drafts are labelled.

## Team review record

- The job records that the canonical SKILL.md and REVIEW.md were read in full, including version/source revision. Conflicts were raised rather than bypassed.
- No agent edited active skills, checks, tools or model settings. No client identity, palette, contact or personal path was copied into shared instructions or samples.
- Every option has full-size and realistic feed/phone evidence. Missing mandatory inspection is incomplete, not a pass; automated checks never constitute human approval.
- Human review records the reviewer, date, exact artifact revision and outcome for every option. Until this exists, label the handover “awaiting human review”; a person alone performs external release.
- Rejection lessons are recorded for the named owner’s generalised GitHub change within one week. Owner assignment and monthly review status are recorded in the package ownership register, not assumed.
- All three review passes are recorded: set/concept comparison, every full-size export, and every feed/phone context view; human review is separate from agent QA.
