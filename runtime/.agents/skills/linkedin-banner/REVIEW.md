# Banner review

Read before work. Inspect the final exported file, not only the design-tool canvas. Record pass, fail, not tested or not applicable with destination, specification source/date and evidence in project notes.

- Review scope matches the request. Screenshots, metadata and exports contain no accidental credentials or private information. Approval and factual verification are recorded separately.
- The input’s personal/business destination was recorded and the corresponding bundled prototype was used.
- The destination is correct: personal profile or Company Page. Group covers are outside this version’s supported scope. Native dimensions, format and byte limit were verified, or an explicit brief-supplied specification is marked unverified.
- Every numbered option in the selected brief is accounted for; any reduced scope was explicit. The export, assigned copy and preview link match for each option.
- Final file count matches the request. Actual file format, extension, colour mode, pixel dimensions and byte size match the chosen specification.
- Every visible word comes from approved copy or an explicitly authorised draft; no invented slogans, proof, credentials or ornamental labels appear.
- Logo and portrait assets are verified. Identity, proportions and brand colours are preserved; no approximated marks or generated likeness substitute for originals.
- Visual quality was reviewed separately from collision checks; essential content was not compressed into a tiny stack merely to clear an overlap.
- One focal idea and clear hierarchy survive at desktop display size and a narrow/mobile simulation.
- The final export was inspected inside the correct profile/Page layout at desktop and mobile sizes, including a narrow phone view; previews and geometry are saved. A proportional resize alone does not satisfy this check.
- Preview settings were held fixed during artwork correction; measured and simulated geometry are distinguished.
- Essential words, logo and subject remain clear of plausible destination-specific avatar/interface overlap and crop risks. Safe-area simulations are labelled as simulations.
- Full-size inspection reveals no spelling defects, rough logo edges, unwanted matte, soft imagery, accidental cutoffs or stretched assets.
- Contrast, typography, image crop and negative space support the actual brief. No miniature website UI, fake metrics or decorative filler weakens the banner.
- Each requested destination was recomposed natively. Variants differ meaningfully where requested and remain consistent in facts and brand.
- The review site contains only the selected client/destination and its requested options; no unused destination route, simulator controls or unrelated sidebar is exposed.
- If a human published a preview, its supplied URL was inspected read-only for the correct export, assets and navigation. Otherwise live checks remain pending; no agent deploy or route removal occurred.
- Editable source, assets and final previews are available. The final export includes all corrections.
- No agent upload occurred. Any human upload/crop verification is claimed only with recorded evidence.
- A human designer reviews native, reduced and phone views before external handover; agent QA is recorded separately.

Wrong destination, altered copy, false claims, distorted identity, unreadable essential content or an uninspected final file prevents a pass. A missing in-context preview, profile-image collision or unreadable mobile view also prevents a readiness pass. Unknown platform limits block a claim of verified upload readiness, not honest delivery of a labelled design draft.

## Package and handover consistency

- Every requested deliverable and variant is accounted for against the brief, with its assigned copy/source and actual export or route. Reduced scope was explicit; no universal variant count was assumed.

## Team review record

- The job records that the canonical SKILL.md and REVIEW.md were read in full, including version/source revision. Conflicts were raised rather than bypassed.
- No agent edited active skills, checks, tools or model settings. No client identity, palette, contact or personal path was copied into shared instructions or samples.
- Every option has full-size and realistic feed/phone evidence. Missing mandatory inspection is incomplete, not a pass; automated checks never constitute human approval.
- Human review records the reviewer, date, exact artifact revision and outcome for every option. Until this exists, label the handover “awaiting human review”; a person alone performs external release.
- Rejection lessons are recorded for the named owner’s generalised GitHub change within one week. Owner assignment and monthly review status are recorded in the package ownership register, not assumed.
- A specific design thesis and coherent art direction are recorded; generic decoration and invented proof were rejected.
