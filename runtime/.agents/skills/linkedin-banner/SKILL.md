# LinkedIn Banner

Create actual cover exports for the requested destination. Read [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md) before starting and before handover. Feed posts and carousels are different deliverables.

For an explicitly requested audit, prompt or specification only, deliver that scope; do not force full production. An explicit supplied-assets-only or no-generation instruction overrides generation defaults. Content approval does not verify factual accuracy; flag unsupported claims instead of silently endorsing or rewriting them.

## Team rules for every job

Read this SKILL.md and REVIEW.md in full before every job. Record the canonical skill path, version and source revision in the client’s job notes. If an instruction is wrong, unclear or conflicts with another rule, raise it with the skill owner; do not work around it or silently select another version. The team governance in [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md) applies to every deliverable and supporting tool.

Keep client names, palettes, contacts, private files and machine-specific paths in the client’s folder, never in reusable skills or samples.

The agent prepares and checks local work only. A human designer must inspect every option at full size and feed/phone size and record approval separately from automated checks. Agents never deploy, publish, upload, host, post or send deliverables, including preview deployments, test emails and real form submissions. Approval to release does not delegate release to a bot. A person performs the release.


## Establish destination and sources

Read the brief, audience, intended impression, approved copy, brand kit and supplied imagery. Determine personal profile or Company Page before choosing a canvas. Group covers are outside this version’s supported scope; explain this if requested, and do not substitute a personal/business template or claim Group verification. Verify current dimensions, accepted formats and upload byte limit against official LinkedIn guidance or the destination's current upload interface; record source and date in the project. Do not silently update the skill when specifications change. If verification is unavailable, use a brief-supplied specification and label its status; do not claim it is current.

Preserve wording, punctuation, URLs and approved assets. Do not invent slogans, credentials, employers, partnerships, metrics, contact details or decorative labels. Keep missing mandatory assets unresolved; do not redraw a logo or substitute a typed name for its official mark. Keep supplied portraits unchanged in identity; use the original file when likeness matters.

Record destination, canvas, final count, copy source, asset provenance and intended use. Inventory every numbered banner option in the source brief before designing. If a selected client task contains three options, produce all three unless the user explicitly limits the run to one option. “One example per skill” selects one client task; it does not silently discard that task’s remaining options. Do not assume every future task has three variants. When neither the brief nor the user specifies a count, create one complete direction.

Keep a simple option-to-copy-to-export checklist in the case notes. Preserve each option’s assigned copy; do not swap messages between variants or substitute three recolours of a single message.

## Compose for a wide crop

Choose one focal idea and a brand-specific visual thesis. A technically unobscured banner can still be a poor design: review hierarchy, readable type, purposeful spacing and brand character before accepting it. Do not solve overlap by squeezing all content into a tiny centred stack or abandoning the visual direction. An extreme horizontal cover cannot carry a miniature landing page. Establish hierarchy, typography, image treatment and safe regions before rendering.

Treat personal-profile avatar overlap, destination UI and responsive trims as risks to inspect. Keep essential content comfortably away from these areas and outer edges. Use extendable backgrounds. Safe-area overlays are conservative simulations, not official fixed crop guarantees; actual crops can change by device and interface state.

Generate art only when useful, using the built-in image tool. Keep production words, logos and official marks out of generated imagery. Typeset approved copy and place verified assets in an editable deterministic composition. Preserve image proportions and logo geometry. Never stretch one destination's export into another: recompose natively.

Build one complete canonical artboard before requested variants. Variants should change the concept or composition meaningfully, not merely colour. For a requested image-only change, preserve typography, copy, logo placement and layout.

### Design thesis and art directions

Before rendering, record one brand-specific visual idea, a controlled palette and accent role, readable type hierarchy, one image treatment, one memorable signature element and an explicit avoid-list. Reject a concept that could be reused unchanged for an unrelated brand.

Choose a coherent direction that fits the brief:

- Editorial authority: confident typography, verified portrait or documentary crop, restrained palette.
- Product precision: supplied product detail, technical grid and disciplined accent; proof only when sourced.
- Founder identity: authentic portrait, approved positioning and a personal visual signature.
- Campaign momentum: bold imagery and one approved campaign message; time-bound CTA only when supplied.
- Quiet premium: purposeful negative space, tactile material and restrained image grading.
- Industrial clarity: structural grid, real process imagery and functional contrast.

Use asymmetry only when it survives cropping. Avoid default AI gradients, floating blobs, meaningless glass cards, fake dashboards/KPIs/partner marks, tiny feature rows, unrelated stock scenes, generated production text and decorative clutter. These are adaptable directions, not compulsory templates or permission to rewrite copy.

## Export and inspect

Export at the verified native canvas and format. Check actual file signature, extension, colour mode, dimensions and byte size against the recorded specification; do not assume a renamed file changes its format. Inspect the export itself at full size, reduced desktop display size and a narrow/mobile simulation. Contact sheets help compare variants but cannot replace individual inspection.

Check exact words, logo geometry, face/product crop, image sharpness, type hierarchy, contrast, margins and interface collisions. Keep intentional decorative edge bleed distinct from accidental clipping. Correct the composition if essential copy is unreadable at display size rather than shrinking it further.

Record simulations honestly. Only a person uploads the cover. Inspect their uploaded result read-only when available; do not upload to prove completion.

## Required in-context check

For a requested LinkedIn prototype, deliver real HTML/CSS for the banner, overlapping profile image, company/person heading, short profile details and recognisable action/navigation rows. Dummy surrounding text is permitted when authorised; do not invent facts inside the banner. Omit right-side recommendations and unrelated page sections unless requested. Keep sliders, calibration controls, technical labels and testing dashboards out of the handoff unless explicitly requested. Record geometry and test details in QA notes, with only material limitations in the handover; do not strip away the profile heading when asked to remove testing clutter.

Before handover, load the final exported banner into a destination-matched profile preview with the profile photo or Company Page logo drawn over it. Inspect desktop and mobile crops, including a narrow phone view. A standalone banner, proportional thumbnail or dimension check is not a substitute.

Inspect a real destination header where accessible before defining crop constraints. Do not compress the design to satisfy invented geometry; source and correct inaccurate preview geometry before assessing artwork. Use measured destination geometry when available; otherwise label the preview as a simulation and record its cover dimensions, crop behaviour and avatar position, size and overlap. Keep those settings fixed while correcting the artwork: do not reduce overlap or change the crop merely to obtain a pass. Never treat simulated coordinates as a universal LinkedIn safe zone.

Check every essential text line, logo and subject against both crop boundaries and the overlaid profile image. If any are obscured, cropped or unreadable, revise the composition, re-export and repeat every required view. Passing bounding-box checks alone does not establish readability or design quality. Preserve locked copy and asset geometry; if they cannot fit legibly, report the conflict rather than silently shrinking or omitting content.

Save the desktop and mobile in-context previews, geometry and review result beside the final export. State separately whether agent QA passed, the preview geometry was calibrated, a human approved the design, and an actual upload was checked. Do not report a banner as ready while a required preview fails or remains uninspected.
