# BIP review

Read before work and inspect the final PDF after export. Record pass, fail, not tested or not applicable for each applicable item in project notes, with page numbers and evidence. An image contact sheet is not a full document review.

- Review scope matches the request. Screenshots, metadata and exports contain no accidental credentials or private information. Approval and factual verification are recorded separately.
- Content approval is established. Every page agrees with the approved copy and source-backed facts; no invented proof, contacts or biographies appear.
- Page count, order, dimensions and format match the brief. A portrait document has not been forced into a slide layout.
- The cover, hierarchy, recurring page system and visual rhythm fit the client and audience. References were used where the direction required them.
- All pages are readable at normal document size or intended presentation size; dense copy was not silently removed or reduced below usable size.
- Margins, gutters, grid, alignment, folios, logo placement and image treatment are coherent. No text collisions, clipping, widows, orphans or stranded headings remain.
- Verified logo and image assets retain proportion and sufficient resolution. No generated text, fake marks or unexplained client/template remnants appear.
- Tables, charts and figures agree with the supplied data and include necessary units, sources and qualifiers.
- PDF metadata uses the supplied document identity rather than internal calendar/job names. No unrelated author, private information or accidental hidden content remains.
- The PDF was reopened and its count, page sizes and order verified. Every rendered page was inspected after the last export.
- Searchable text and fonts were checked where applicable. If flattened, that limitation is disclosed and editable source is included.
- Requested slide PNGs agree with the PDF. Export files are non-empty and necessary linked assets accompany the editable source.
- Print-specific size, bleed, safe area, colour and resolution were checked against supplied printer requirements when applicable; screen review alone is not print approval.
- A human designer reads every final page before external handover. Record this review distinctly from the agent's inspection.

Wrong facts, altered approved copy, missing pages, unreadable text, broken assets or an uninspected PDF prevent a pass. Missing content may be shown only as an explicitly identified review draft. Agents never send or publish; a person handles release.

## Package and handover consistency

- Every requested deliverable and variant is accounted for against the brief, with its assigned copy/source and actual export or route. Reduced scope was explicit; no universal variant count was assumed.

## Team review record

- The job records that the canonical SKILL.md and REVIEW.md were read in full, including version/source revision. Conflicts were raised rather than bypassed.
- No agent edited active skills, checks, tools or model settings. No client identity, palette, contact or personal path was copied into shared instructions or samples.
- Every option has full-size and realistic feed/phone evidence. Missing mandatory inspection is incomplete, not a pass; automated checks never constitute human approval.
- Human review records the reviewer, date, exact artifact revision and outcome for every option. Until this exists, label the handover “awaiting human review”; a person alone performs external release.
- Rejection lessons are recorded for the named owner’s generalised GitHub change within one week. Owner assignment and monthly review status are recorded in the package ownership register, not assumed.
