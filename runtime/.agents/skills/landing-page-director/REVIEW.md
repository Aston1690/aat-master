# Landing page review

Read before work; apply to the final real browser output. In project QA notes record pass, fail, not tested or not applicable with a reason and evidence for each applicable check. A build result or numerical design score does not replace inspection.

## Direction and fidelity

- Review scope matches the request. Screenshots, metadata and exports contain no accidental credentials or private information. Approval and factual verification are recorded separately.
- The brief identifies audience, offer, primary action, sources and locked constraints.
- Required section references exist and were inspected for a new visual direction. Their paths and agent-selected/user-approved status are accurate. A clone or narrow edit records its supplied visual target instead.
- The coded page visibly follows the selected direction, with purposeful imagery, coherent type and varied section rhythm.
- The first viewport has clear message/action hierarchy; the logo is proportionate and the final conversion section has sufficient emphasis.
- Exact copy, source facts, legal wording, logo geometry and approved template constraints survive. No invented claims, fake dashboards or unrelated client details remain.
- No repeated decorative cards, unsupported proof, accidental white logo box, overcrowded hero, tiny body text or meaningless filler weakens the result.
- Supplied quality benchmarks, reported defect widths, shared/structured copy, loaded media, metadata and hidden control states were checked where relevant. An explicit no-generation instruction is honoured.

## Actual behaviour

- Project build, type, lint and tests ran where available, with real results recorded.
- The full rendered page was inspected at 1440, 1024, 768, 390 and 320 px where supported; record actual viewport widths and exceptions.
- Fonts, imagery, line breaks, final CTA and footer were checked. No clipping, overflow or hidden required copy remains.
- Links, menu, buttons, form states, media and other present controls were exercised. Unconfigured integrations do not claim success.
- Keyboard focus, semantics, labels and contrast were checked; core content survives script failure.
- Planned motion was watched on desktop/mobile and reduced motion tested separately. A still screenshot cannot establish animation quality.
- No agent production lead, payment, message, asset upload or deployment was triggered.

## Local integration and human-release handover

- Missing CTA destinations, actual downloads, backend details and intended Vercel target were requested; answers are mapped to the relevant routes/controls.
- Integration code and validation/loading/error/success states were checked through isolated local mocks, with external side effects disabled. Local mocks are not reported as real delivery.
- The human-release handover identifies the source revision, target, required configuration names, pending facts and the external submission/receipt/fulfilment checks a person must perform.
- Human approval, human deployment and real submission results are recorded only when evidenced. An agent did not perform release or external submissions.
- After a human supplies the deployed URL, read-only inspection is recorded separately; until then, deployment/live checks remain pending and the output is not called live-ready.

## Revision and handover

- Final screenshots reflect the latest source/build, not a stale preview.
- Rejected treatments have not returned elsewhere; corrections address their cause without unrelated changes.
- Unresolved facts, placeholders and unperformed tests are disclosed. Material gaps prevent a client-ready claim.
- Source, assets, reference images, run instructions and QA evidence are present.
- A human designer reviews full-page and phone views before external handover; record this separately from agent selection. Only a person publishes after the required human review.

## Package and handover consistency

- Every requested deliverable and variant is accounted for against the brief, with its assigned copy/source and actual export or route. Reduced scope was explicit; no universal variant count was assumed.
- Dependency availability and any fallback used are recorded honestly. Missing required capabilities or project-mandated workflows are disclosed.
- Independent-review evidence, when applicable, remains immutable for its submitted revision. New evidence is submitted as a new revision through the supported review process, and the verdict matches the delivered source.

## Team review record

- The job records that the canonical SKILL.md and REVIEW.md were read in full, including version/source revision. Conflicts were raised rather than bypassed.
- No agent edited active skills, checks, tools or model settings. No client identity, palette, contact or personal path was copied into shared instructions or samples.
- Every option has full-size and realistic feed/phone evidence. Missing mandatory inspection is incomplete, not a pass; automated checks never constitute human approval.
- Human review records the reviewer, date, exact artifact revision and outcome for every option. Until this exists, label the handover “awaiting human review”; a person alone performs external release.
- Rejection lessons are recorded for the named owner’s generalised GitHub change within one week. Owner assignment and monthly review status are recorded in the package ownership register, not assumed.
