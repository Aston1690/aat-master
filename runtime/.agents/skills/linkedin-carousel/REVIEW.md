# Carousel review

Read before work. Inspect every final slide and rendered PDF page. Record pass, fail, not tested or not applicable with slide numbers and evidence in project notes.

- Every slide, including cover, statistic, quotation and closing slides, contains a prominent relevant photograph or substantive illustration; no text-only slide or token icon/logo/shape stands in for imagery.
- Image sources or generation prompts and saved assets are recorded. Generated scenes are not passed off as documentary evidence of real client people, places or events.
- Review scope matches the request. Screenshots, metadata and exports contain no accidental credentials or private information. Approval and factual verification are recorded separately.
- There are exactly N ordered PNGs and one N-page PDF for the planned N slides. Numeric sequence, page size and ordering match; no duplicate, omitted or separate per-slide PDF replaces the set.
- The narrative advances one coherent argument. Each slide has a useful role and the final slide resolves the promise without invented proof or CTA copy.
- Approved words, punctuation and factual sources match on every slide. Authorised condensed copy has the required acceptance before final use.
- Only permitted pagination and authorised labels are added. Draft placeholders remain visibly identifiable on each affected slide; no fabricated facts appear.
- Typography, palette, image grade, margins, logos and pagination are coherent. Composition and density vary usefully without ignoring an approved template.
- Every slide is readable at full size and roughly 360 px display width. Supporting text and qualifiers remain attached to their claims.
- Copy, branding and pagination have clear space and adequate contrast. Critical subjects are not obscured; an improvised patch does not conceal a failed composition.
- Generated artwork contains no production words, fake logos or false UI. Exact copy and official assets were composed separately.
- Seamless elements align at adjacent boundaries when that treatment is used; no critical text, faces or logos are split.
- The PDF was reopened, counted and rendered. Every page visually matches the corresponding final PNG; text/searchability status and metadata are correct.
- Editable source and necessary assets are present. No internal calendar name, unrelated client identity or accidental private information appears in metadata or output.
- Current platform requirements were checked before any claim of upload readiness. No agent publishing or public asset hosting occurred.
- A human designer reads all slides at full and feed/phone size before external handover; record actual review separately from agent QA.

Missing slides, wrong order, altered copy, unsupported claims, unreadable text, fake marks or an uninspected PDF prevents a pass. A contact sheet alone cannot establish completion.

Missing required imagery, placeholder artwork, or imagery too small or obscured to communicate at feed size prevents a pass. An earlier approval of text-only work does not waive the owner’s current imagery requirement.

## Package and handover consistency

- Every requested deliverable and variant is accounted for against the brief, with its assigned copy/source and actual export or route. Reduced scope was explicit; no universal variant count was assumed.
- Approved accompanying captions, hashtags and links are preserved and paired with the correct final files or slide set in the caption handover; absent captions are disclosed and unapproved drafts are labelled.

## Team review record

- The job records that the canonical SKILL.md and REVIEW.md were read in full, including version/source revision. Conflicts were raised rather than bypassed.
- No agent edited active skills, checks, tools or model settings. No client identity, palette, contact or personal path was copied into shared instructions or samples.
- Every option has full-size and realistic feed/phone evidence. Missing mandatory inspection is incomplete, not a pass; automated checks never constitute human approval.
- Human review records the reviewer, date, exact artifact revision and outcome for every option. Until this exists, label the handover “awaiting human review”; a person alone performs external release.
- Rejection lessons are recorded for the named owner’s generalised GitHub change within one week. Owner assignment and monthly review status are recorded in the package ownership register, not assumed.
- The client-local creative bible is present and applied consistently across every slide.
